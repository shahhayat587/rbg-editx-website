How do I place an order?

Choose a service or package, fill in the project form, and tap Continue to WhatsApp. Your details will be prepared in a WhatsApp message. Check it and press Send to contact us.

Are the displayed prices final?

Our listed package rates are Basic Rs. 500, Pro Rs. 1,000 and Premium Rs. 1,500. For longer or more complex edits, request a custom quote before work begins.

How long does delivery take?

Our package delivery targets are Basic: 6 hours, Pro: 12 hours and Premium: within 1 day. Confirm the exact start time and deadline over WhatsApp before work begins.

Can I request revisions?

Yes. Tell us what changes you may need before placing an order. The number of included revisions and any additional charges will be agreed for your specific project.

Can you edit vertical Reels, Shorts and TikToks?

Yes. You can request a vertical 9:16 video, a square 1:1 video or a horizontal 16:9 video. Mention the intended platform and dimensions in your brief.

Can you make Alight Motion CC + XML reels?

Yes. Explore the After Result gallery to see a finished Alight Motion reel featuring color grading and effects. Share a reference if you have a specific style in mind.

How do I share my raw clips or references?

Paste a reference URL in the order form if you have one. Once you connect on WhatsApp, we can agree on a suitable method for sharing your raw footage. The website form itself does not upload video files.

What are the payment methods?

Payment method and schedule are confirmed directly on WhatsApp before work starts. This website does not collect payments or card details.
